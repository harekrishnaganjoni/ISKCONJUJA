PHONE REMINDERS - SET-UP (one time, about 10 minutes)
Hare Krishna Dan. Nothing here needs a computer program, only your browser.

WHAT MEMBERS WILL GET
Each member chooses, in My profile > Phone reminders, what they want:
  - programs and special events: the evening before (6 pm) and/or a few hours before (they pick 1 to 5 hours)
  - ekadashi and other fasts: the evening before, with the break-fast time
  - festivals and acharya days: the evening before and/or the morning of
  - their own dates (birthday, initiation, anniversary): the evening before and/or a greeting on the morning
The reminder pops up on their phone like any app notification, even when the app is closed.
It costs nothing. No SMS or Telegram account is needed.

STEP 1 - the database (Supabase)
  1. Open Supabase > your project > SQL Editor > New query.
  2. Open the attached file  35-phone-reminders-run-thirty-fifth.sql  in Notepad, select all, copy.
  3. Paste into the SQL Editor and press Run. It must say Success.

STEP 2 - wait for the website to update
  The new files are already pushed. Cloudflare updates the site by itself in a few minutes.

STEP 3 - make the keys (admin portal)
  1. Sign in to the admin portal > Shop & site > Settings.
  2. Scroll down to "Phone reminders" and open it.
  3. Press "Create the reminder keys".
  4. A SECRET KEY appears (a line of about 43 letters). Press "Copy the secret key". Keep this window open until step 4 is done.
     (It is shown only once. If you lose it, just press the button again to make new keys.)

STEP 4 - give the sender its two secrets (Cloudflare)
  1. Open Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > iskconjuja > Settings > Variables and Secrets.
  2. Add a SECRET named exactly:   VAPID_PRIVATE      Value: paste the secret key from step 3.
  3. Add a second SECRET named exactly:   SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY
     Value: Supabase > Project Settings > API > "service_role" key (press Reveal, copy). 
     This key is very powerful. Never paste it in a chat or email. Only here.
  4. Press Deploy / Save.
  5. Settings > Triggers should show a schedule "*/15 * * * *" (every 15 minutes).

STEP 5 - check it works
  1. Wait 15 minutes. In admin > Settings > Phone reminders the line should say "The sender last ran: ...".
  2. On your phone, open the member app (installed if possible; on an iPhone it must be added to the Home Screen first),
     go to My profile > Phone reminders, press "Switch reminders on for this phone" and tap Allow.
  3. Choose what you want and press "Save my choices".
  4. At the next reminder time (for example 6 pm the evening before a Tuesday program) your phone will buzz.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - "The sender last ran" line shows "problem: ..." : send me that exact line.
  - Nothing at all shown after 30 minutes: step 4 is missing one of the two secrets, or the names are not exactly as written.
  - Members must switch reminders on again (once) if you ever press "Make new keys".
