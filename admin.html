How to print it (double-sided)
  1. Click Print A4 sheet. Two pages open in the print window: page 1 is the fronts, page 2 is the backs. Each page holds 10 cards (2 across, 5 down) with a 2 mm gap.
  2. Paper size A4. Scale 100% (not “Fit to page”). Margins None. Turn Background graphics ON, otherwise the dark back and the colours will not print.
  3. Choose Two-sided and Flip on long edge (the printer may call it “Portrait, long-edge binding”). If your printer cannot print both sides, print page 1, put the sheet back in the tray with the blank side facing the print side, then print page 2 only. Test on plain paper first and hold it up to the light to check that fronts and backs line up.
  4. Use thick paper (250–300 gsm) for real cards. Cut along the 2 mm gaps, or print and take it to a print shop to cut.
  5. Most printers cannot print right to the edge. These cards sit at least 7 mm from the paper edge, so nothing is cut off.
