Our Weekly Rhythm

Feed Your Soul. Join the Circle.

Two evenings of music and community in Juja, plus regular trips to the main temple in Nairobi — there is always a space waiting for you here.

Save My Spot
Tuesdays & Fridays, 5:30 – 7:00 PM

This Week's Programs

Held at Anthusia Apartments, Muraya Access Road, Juja.

  • 🎸Tuesday — Music, a talk or open discussion (often with a guest speaker visiting from the ISKCON Nairobi/Ngara temple), and a free vegetarian feast.
  • 📖Friday — Music, group book reading & discussion in place of a talk, and a free vegetarian feast.

Every Sunday

Visit the Main Temple — Nairobi (Ngara)

We don't run a local Sunday program — instead we organize group trips to the ISKCON temple in Ngara, Nairobi. The group usually travels Saturday evening or Sunday morning around 6:30 AM, returning Sunday afternoon or evening.

NAIROBI (NGARA) SUNDAY SCHEDULE

  • 📖7:45 AM — Class
  • 🍲9:00 AM — Breakfast
  • 🎸11:00 AM — Music
  • 🕯12:30 PM — Worship Ceremony
  • 📖1:00 PM — Class
  • 🍲2:00 PM — Lunch
