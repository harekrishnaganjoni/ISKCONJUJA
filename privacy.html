Your Data

Privacy Policy

Plain language, not legal jargon. Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Short version: we collect only what we need to run programs, the shop, and the member area; we never sell your data; you can ask us to see or delete what we hold on you at any time.

Who we are

ISKCON JUJA (the Hare Krishna Community Centre, Juja) operates this website. For any question about your data, message us on WhatsApp or call .

What we collect, and why

If you...We collectWhy
Message us on WhatsAppWhatever you send us, via WhatsApp itselfTo reply to you
Post a testimonial or message board postYour name (optional — you can post anonymously) and your messageTo show it publicly, as you asked us to
Are given a member login by an adminYour email, and anything you do in the member area (course progress, etc.)To run your account and track your own progress
Take a courseYour progress and quiz results, saved under an anonymous guest code kept in your own browser (no name or email needed), and (if the course offers one) a certificate in your nameTo track your learning and issue certificates

Consent

We only collect what you choose to give us at the point you give it — for example, ticking a box before posting your name on the message board, or an admin creating your member account with your knowledge. We don't collect data silently in the background.

Where it's stored

Our database is hosted by Supabase (a data processor, not a data controller — they process data on our instructions, they don't use it for their own purposes). Security rules in our database mean your private data (like your course progress) is visible only to you and our admins, never to other visitors.

We never sell your data

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone, ever.

Messages and reminders

If we ever send you reminders about events or programs (by SMS, Telegram, or calendar invite), it's only because you asked for them when registering, and you choose the channel yourself. You can always ask us to stop.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, you can ask us at any time to:

  • show you what data we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your account and data
  • stop sending you reminders or messages

Just message us on WhatsApp to ask. We'll respond as quickly as we can.

Children

Our programs are aimed at university students and adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
