Welcome to the Hare Krishna Community Centre! We are a student-focused space right here in Juja, built to give you a real break from the usual campus chaos, stress, and burnout.

Think of this as your spot to recharge, connect with grounded people, and explore life beyond the routine. Whether you're dropping by for open discussions on big life questions, learning simple meditation tools to clear your mind, or just pulling up a chair for good, wholesome food and solid company, you're always welcome.

No pressure, no pitch — just a positive space for anyone looking for authentic friendship and higher purpose. Pull up, chill, and feel at home!

What to Expect at a Program

Your First Visit

No experience needed. Just bring yourself and an open mind. The schedule below is only for our structured programs — outside of these times, you're always welcome to drop by the centre just to chill, hang out, and learn at your own pace, no rigid program required.

🎸

1. Music & Meditation

Every Tuesday and Friday from 5:30 PM, we open with live acoustic music and collective chanting. It is the perfect way to clear your head and shake off the stress of the week.

💡

2. Talk or Book Discussion

Tuesdays feature an open discussion breaking down ancient wisdom into practical tools — often with a guest speaker visiting from the ISKCON Nairobi (Ngara) temple. Fridays, we read and discuss a book together as a group.

🍽️

3. Free Vegetarian Feast

We wrap up around 7:00 PM by sharing a freshly cooked vegetarian meal together. It is completely free, entirely homemade, and a great chance to make new friends.
