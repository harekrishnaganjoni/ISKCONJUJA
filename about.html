Srila Prabhupada, our founder

In 1965, at the age of 69, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada boarded a cargo ship from India to New York City. He suffered two heart attacks at sea and arrived with less than seven dollars in his pocket.

 Continue the storyShow less

What he did carry was an unshakeable faith in his spiritual master's instruction and a trunk filled with his translations of ancient Indian scripture.

While the Western world was undergoing massive social upheaval, Srila Prabhupada quietly launched a spiritual counter-revolution. He sat beneath an elm tree in New York's Tompkins Square Park and began the Hare Krishna chant — capturing the hearts of young seekers looking for real substance.

A Global Legacy Built in Eleven Years: He traveled the earth 14 times, establishing over 108 temples across six continents. He translated and published over 80 volumes including the Bhagavad Gita As It Is. He pioneered Food for Life, the world's largest vegetarian food relief network.

Our centre in Juja

The Hare Krishna Community Centre in Juja began in the closing days of the COVID-19 pandemic, as a spiritual sanctuary for university students in a town known for party, gambling and addiction culture.

 Continue the storyShow less

The origin of the centre traces back to the global mission launched by Srila Prabhupada, who brought the movement to East Africa in 1971, visiting Nairobi and Mombasa to establish roots based on universal equality and selfless service. ISKCON's primary East African hub is the Sri Sri Radha Bankebhari Ji Temple in Nairobi's Ngara neighborhood.

Local practitioners began hosting intimate house gatherings, inviting students for philosophical discussions and shared meals alongside experienced practitioners like Sriman Govinda. Curiosity spread across campus when members began wearing traditional devotional attire, sparking everyday conversations with peers searching for substance and mental clarity amidst the noise of student life.

With guidance and sponsorship supported by the Nairobi temple, well-wishers helped secure a dedicated local space, officially establishing the Hare Krishna Community Centre in Juja. Carrying forward Prabhupada's original vision, the centre established a vibrant routine of mantra meditation, philosophical study and interactive student engagement — offering youth a welcoming home for personal growth, authentic friendship, and higher purpose.

Today, the Juja centre forms part of a vibrant, interconnected regional network across East Africa. From established urban centres in Kampala, Jinja, and Dar-es-Salaam, to Kenyan coastal and upcountry hubs in Mombasa (Nyali and Ganjoni), Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru, and an emerging centre in Kitale, the movement continues to expand. This network is further grounded by sustainable agricultural initiative hubs — such as the Thika farm in Ndula/Ngoliba and the Kabaa farm — offering communities places for eco-friendly living, spiritual retreat, and practical service.

Questions
Can anyone visit ISKCON JUJA?

Yes. Visitors, families, students, and seekers are welcome. Join us Tuesdays & Fridays from 5:30 PM, or check our Events page for full program details.

Do I need to be Hindu to attend?

No. ISKCON programs are open to anyone interested in spiritual learning, music, food, community, and service — regardless of background.

What happens during a Tuesday or Friday program?

Both evenings run 5:30–7:00 PM with music and a free vegetarian feast. Tuesdays usually feature a talk or discussion, often with a guest speaker visiting from the Nairobi (Ngara) temple. Fridays are for reading and discussing a book together as a group.

Do you have a Sunday program?

Not locally — instead, we organize group visits to the main ISKCON temple in Ngara, Nairobi. The group usually travels Saturday evening or Sunday morning around 6:30 AM, and returns Sunday afternoon or evening. See our Events page for the Nairobi temple's Sunday schedule.

Can I bring children?

Absolutely. ISKCON JUJA is family-friendly, and youth activities are a growing part of our community.

How can I help or volunteer?

Visit our Get Involved page to join the cooking crew, music team, event setup, or support through donations and in-kind gifts.
